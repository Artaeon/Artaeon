# Raphael René Lugmayr

Christian entrepreneur and software developer building digital systems in Austria.

I design, build, and operate software for businesses, municipalities, and schools. I co-founded [Stoicera Group](https://stoicera.com), serve as CTO of [Schulmittag](https://schulmittag.at), and publish [Austrian Business](https://austrianbusiness.at).

My public work focuses on dependable developer tools, local-first software, and interpretable AI systems. I value clear ownership, open formats, and software that remains useful long after its first release.

## How I work

- Build for real use, not for demos.
- Prefer open formats and systems people can own.
- Treat security, privacy, and maintenance as product features.
- Take responsibility for what reaches production.

My Christian faith shapes this work: with care, integrity, and service to others.
